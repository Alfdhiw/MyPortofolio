SIMONE

An online internship registration system for Semarang District Court.

Overview

SIMONE is a web-based internship registration system for Pengadilan Negeri Semarang, or Semarang District Court. Its public introduction explains the service before applicants enter the account-based areas of the system.

The challenge

An institutional application needs to identify the organization and explain its purpose before asking visitors to sign in. The first screen also needs to distinguish learning about the internship service from accessing an existing account.

My role & scope

I worked as a full stack programmer on the web application. The portfolio walkthrough uses the public introduction, rather than applicant records or authenticated screens, to explain the entry experience.

Interface approach

The screen pairs a clear institutional heading and a short service description with an illustration. A green background unifies the page, while white text and controls define the reading order. Login is separated into the header, and Read more provides a second route from the introduction.

Key features

The public page identifies online internship registration as the core service. Its introduction also lists attendance, grades, available places, and certificates as parts of the wider internship experience.

Read more and Login serve different entry needs: understanding the service and accessing an account. Both remain visible without competing with the main institutional message.

Outcome

The public screen gives applicants an identifiable starting point, explains the system’s purpose, and separates information from account access. The introduction communicates the wider internship service without exposing applicant records in the public presentation.
