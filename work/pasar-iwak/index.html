Overview

Pasar Iwak is a marketplace for freshwater fish and related products, with an emphasis on the Kendal community. The original homepage brings buyers, fish farmers, and sellers into a shared product-discovery experience.

The challenge

The platform needs to make different types of discovery understandable within the same storefront. Buyers may start with fish, feed, or seed, while others may want to find a seller or explore an auction. Each route needs a recognizable place in the homepage hierarchy.

My role & scope

I contributed as a full stack programmer. This presentation focuses on the marketplace homepage preserved in the project screenshot, including its category navigation, search entry point, account actions, and promotional areas.

Interface approach

The header keeps product navigation, search, the cart indicator, and account actions together. A secondary group of links separates supporting information, marketplace partners, and the community forum from the main shopping navigation.

Below the header, a large fish-farming image introduces the marketplace, while smaller panels give auctions and seller recommendations their own space. Green and teal accents connect the brand identity with the discovery controls.

Key features

The captured interface includes Ikan, Pakan, and Bibit categories, a product search field, Masuk and Daftar actions, a cart indicator, and entry points for Lelang Ikan and Rekomendasi Penjual. Supporting panels also introduce product quality, service, transactions, and contacting sellers.

Outcome

The original homepage brings product discovery and seller discovery into one marketplace introduction. The preserved interface shows how category navigation, search, and promotional routes can coexist without making every visitor follow the same starting path.
